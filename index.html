What I do

Three ways I help teams build better AI

01

Evaluate

I rank and compare AI-generated responses for accuracy, instruction following, reasoning and clarity, using detailed rubrics and clear written justifications.

02

Stress-test

I write advanced adversarial prompts that models can't reliably solve, then document failure patterns, ambiguous cases and edge cases so they feed into model improvement.

03

Build

I design AI-enabled workflows, data models and automation across AWS, Azure and Google Cloud, turning ambiguous requirements into explicit system rules.
